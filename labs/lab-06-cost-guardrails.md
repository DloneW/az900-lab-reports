## Objective

Configure cost management controls in Azure to prevent unexpected spend — including budgets, alerts, and spending thresholds.

## Core Concepts Covered

- Using **Azure Cost Management + Billing** to track spend
- Creating a **budget** scoped to a subscription or resource group
- Configuring **cost alerts** at defined spending thresholds
- Understanding forecasted vs. actual cost views
- Reviewing cost breakdowns by resource/service

## Relevance

Cost guardrails are a governance control, but they double as an early-warning system for security incidents — a sudden, unexplained spike in resource usage or spend is often a symptom of compromised credentials being used to spin up resources (e.g., cryptomining). Understanding how cost alerting works ties directly into anomaly detection thinking.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Azure Cost Management configuration
- Budget and alert threshold setup
- Cost anomaly awareness as a security signal
